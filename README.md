# 🌍 Automated Global Breaking News YouTube Shorts Bot

Fully automated 24/7 AI-driven pipeline that tracks breaking news across the **USA, UK, Germany, France, Italy, India, Japan, Singapore, Sweden, and Switzerland**, identifies the single highest-heat viral story, synthesizes editorial visuals and anchor narration, renders a 1080x1920 Short with live breaking banners, and publishes 2 Shorts daily directly to YouTube.

---

## ⚡ Highlights
* **Target Nations Monitored:** USA, UK, Germany, France, Italy, India, Japan, Singapore, Sweden, Switzerland.
* **Auto Heat Detection:** Google Gemini 2.0 Flash scans real-time headlines and rates virality.
* **Photojournalism Visuals:** Pollinations.ai / Flux 9:16 vertical editorial documentary imagery.
* **Anchor Voice:** Neural anchor narration (`en-US-AndrewNeural`) with word-accurate styled captions and live red breaking news ticker banner.
* **Automated Schedule:** Runs 2x daily (11:00 AM & 11:00 PM PKT) on GitHub Actions + Instant Manual Trigger for breaking news.
